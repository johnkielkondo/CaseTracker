The Digest Tracker Project

A focused project with a simple purpose: make the everyday work of organizing case digests feel clearer and easier to return to.

Behind the experience.

The design takes its cues from well-used law books: deep blue covers, warm paper, gold lettering, and a place for every chapter. The scroll interaction turns that familiar object into an introduction to your study space.

Built to stay approachable.

The interactive book uses Three.js and TypeScript, built with Vite. Its leather texture, gold lettering, and paper are generated locally, while curved page geometry responds to scrolling. Supabase powers accounts and saved study data. Keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences remain supported.
